BUSINESS & TAX SETTINGS

The GST rate is used for every GST estimate in this app (Australia's standard rate is 10%). The cents-per-km rate is used for the dashboard comparison, capped at 5,000 business km a year; check the current rate on ato.gov.au. Rideshare drivers are generally required to register for GST regardless of income.
